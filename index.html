AI Workflow

How I build with AI

1) Prompts

Each product starts with role-based prompts for a specific student task.

“Act as a campus career coach. Review this resume for internship readiness. Give concise rewrites with measurable outcomes and plain-language feedback.”

2) Iterations

I test output quality across majors, skill levels, and job types.

“Rewrite for a first-year student with no internship history. Keep language direct, specific, and realistic.”

3) Improvements

  • Reduced vague outputs with structured response templates.
  • Improved relevance using job-specific context injection.
  • Added rubric checks for clarity, accuracy, and usefulness.
