About Me

I’m Guna Banka, an AI Product Engineer focused on practical outcomes.

I build practical AI tools that solve real problems for students applying to internships and entry-level roles.

This page explains the direction behind the portfolio so visitors can understand both the person behind the work and the product thinking behind it.

Who I want to become

  • An AI Product Engineer who ships useful products, not just demos.
  • A builder who explains AI clearly through strong product logic and practical execution.
  • A junior talent profile that recruiters can evaluate quickly, while still feeling like a real person behind the portfolio.

What kind of work I do

  • Resume improvement tools with AI feedback and targeted rewrites.
  • Job description analysis tools with clear, plain-language outputs.
  • Cover letter feedback tools that improve real application materials.

Portfolio identity and tone

The portfolio has a consistent identity built around students, the job market, and AI tools. The primary audience is students, and the secondary audience is recruiters evaluating junior AI and product talent.

The tone is clear, practical, and usefulness-first. It avoids hype and vague language while still leaving room for personality, reflection, and a sense of why the work matters to me.
