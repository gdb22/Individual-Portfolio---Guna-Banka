See how long student debt could stay with you.

This website helps students enter a loan balance and get a simple repayment estimate based on debt ranges, turning abstract loan numbers into something easier to understand.

What this page does

Built to help students understand debt more clearly

  • Lets visitors enter their own loan balance.
  • Shows a quick repayment estimate using debt ranges.
  • Frames borrowing in a practical, clearer way.

How this estimate was calculated

Right at the 2025 average balance

Assumes a 6.53% fixed APR benchmark. At that same rate and term, the 2025 average balance would cost about $337/mo.

Current estimate uses the $20,000 to $39,999 balance band and a longer repayment window than the standard 10-year plan.

Why This Matters

Debt becomes easier to understand when it feels personal

Instead of showing only charts, this version lets students enter their own number and see how repayment can stretch over time as debt grows.

What Changed

Focused experience, simpler tool

This version keeps the experience centered on one clear task: helping students estimate repayment in a simple, usable format.
