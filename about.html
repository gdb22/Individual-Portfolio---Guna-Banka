About Me

I’m Guna Banka, an AI Product Engineer focused on practical outcomes.

I want to build practical AI tools that solve real problems for students applying to internships and entry-level roles.

This page explains the direction behind the portfolio so visitors can understand the identity behind the work.

Who I want to become

  • An AI Product Engineer who ships useful tools, not demos.
  • A builder who explains AI simply, with clear product logic.
  • A junior talent profile that recruiters can evaluate quickly.

What kind of work I do

  • Resume quality tools with AI feedback and rewrites.
  • Job description analysis tools with plain-language outputs.
  • Cover letter feedback tools that improve real submissions.

Portfolio identity and tone

Consistent identity: students + job market + AI tools. Primary audience is students. Secondary audience is recruiters looking for junior AI/product talent.

Tone is clear, practical, and usefulness-first. No hype. No vague language. Every project explains the problem, workflow, and improvement steps.
